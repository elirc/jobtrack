# 01 — Codebase Cartography

Mapping skills: how to take an unfamiliar repo and, within hours, know its shape, its
vocabulary, its reading order, and its critical flows. Practiced here on a repo small
enough to map completely — so you learn what "completely mapped" feels like and can
recognize how far from it you are on a big repo.

Interview relevance: "walk me through a codebase you learned recently" is a standard
mid-level prompt. This module *is* the rehearsal for it.
